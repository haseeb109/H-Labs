 TITLE RULES — TRIAL TALES  (Courtroom-Karma formula, owner's master v2 adapted)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
VIEWER PSYCHOLOGY. The viewer wants one emotional transaction: "Show me a horrible
person doing a horrible thing - then show me an authority figure CRUSHING them."
Sell catharsis, not information: outrage trigger + audacity + authority dominance
+ an unanswered curiosity gap ("what exactly does the judge do?").

STRUCTURE. [SETUP] [SEPARATOR] [PAYOFF with {JUDGE_NAME}]
  SEPARATOR: " — " (default) or " | " for a 3-part title:
  [Crime/Setup] — [Audacity middle] | {JUDGE_NAME} [Payoff]

SETUP FORMULAS (pick what the transcript supports)
  1 Shocking Act:      He/She [violent verb] [vulnerable victim / specific detail]
  2 Repeat Offender:   Broke Probation [N] Times / [N] Years Of Crime
  3 Dollar Amount:     Stole $[X] From [Place]
  4 Audacity:          Still Thought She'll Walk Free / Thought It Was a Joke
  5 Unforgivable:      What He Did to [Vulnerable Victim] is Unforgivable
  6 Adjective+Subject: [Arrogant/Lying/Careless/Cold-Blooded] [Defendant/Woman/Mom] [Action]
                       (never as the very first word of the title - start with the subject or act)
  7 The Beg:           [Subject] Begs For Mercy / Begged For [X] After [Crime]
  8 Walked In Smiling: [Subject] Walked Into Court Smiling
  9 Lied To Court:     She Lied In Court About [X]
 10 Outsmart Attempt:  Tried To Outsmart {JUDGE_NAME} / Tried to Fool {JUDGE_NAME}
                       ({JUDGE_NAME} sits in the setup here, so not again in the payoff)

PAYOFF FORMULAS (one per title, ONE bank verb in CAPS)
  A Brutal Sentence:   {JUDGE_NAME} SLAMS Maximum Prison
  B Humiliation:       {JUDGE_NAME} HUMILIATES Her in Court
  C Destruction:       {JUDGE_NAME} TEARS Her Apart / RIPS Into Him
  D Curiosity:         {JUDGE_NAME} SHOCKED Everyone With Her Decision
                       (the original "Makes a Decision No One Expected" has no CAPS word -
                       every title needs exactly one)
  E Instant Regret:    Then {JUDGE_NAME} Made Her Regret It INSTANTLY
  F Refusal:           {JUDGE_NAME} REFUSES and Shows No Mercy
  G Reveal:            {JUDGE_NAME} Pulled Her 1997 Case & EVERYTHING Changed (the number
                       must be in the transcript)
  H Last Hope:         {JUDGE_NAME} DESTROYS His Last Hope
  I The Ending:        {JUDGE_NAME} ENDED It With Maximum Prison  ("Maximum" only if the
                       transcript says maximum)
  J Verdict Shock:     {JUDGE_NAME} SHUTS DOWN His Excuse Instantly

POWER-VERB BANK (the CAPS word for karma; also allowed in other styles)
  SLAMS · TEARS · HUMILIATES · DESTROYS · RAGES · RIPS · REFUSES · ENDED · SLAPS ·
  SHUTS DOWN · BRUTAL · SHOCKED · REJECTS
EMPHASIS LIST (other styles may use one of these as the CAPS word instead)
  EVERYTHING · INSTANTLY · NEVER · SILENT · LAST · ONE · ZERO · AGAIN
HOOK WORDS (first 40 characters)
  stole · beat · lied · begs · smiling · arrogant · broke · probation · times · years ·
  mom · toddler · victim · walk free · outsmart · fool · disrespects · crash · drunk

LENGTH. Target 65-95 characters, absolute max 100. If too long, cut in this order:
  filler conjunctions → non-trigger adjectives → redundant "in court". Never cut the
  power verb, {JUDGE_NAME}, the number or the audacity phrase.
CENSOR. "sex offender" → "Se* Offender"; keep drug and violence words neutral.

CALIBRATION (rhythm only - never copy):
  He Beats a Doctor In Hospital After Being Denied Treatment — {JUDGE_NAME} SLAMS Him Hard
  Mom Of 5 Stole $40K+ for Drugs — Still Thought She'll Walk Free | {JUDGE_NAME} TEARS Her Apart
  She Thought She'd Walk Free — Then {JUDGE_NAME} Pulled Her 1997 Case & Everything Changed
  Broke Probation 13 Times — Mom Screams for Mercy | {JUDGE_NAME} Doesn't Budge
  Cold-Blooded Killer Begs for Probation — {JUDGE_NAME} REFUSES, Hands Down Maximum Prison
Cadence: short punchy setup → em-dash beat → optional audacity layer → pipe → the
judge's CAPS verdict.
Note: some calibration lines (e.g. "Doesn't Budge", "Cold-Blooded" as the first word)
break the hard rules above. When a calibration line and a rule disagree, the RULE wins.
