 TITLE RULES — DOCUMENTARY STYLES
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Pattern: {JUDGE_NAME} [POWER VERB] [LABEL] [stakes / number] – [twist]
  POWER VERBS: DELIVERS, SLAMS, SHUTS DOWN, EXPOSES, CATCHES, STOPS, WARNS, ENDS
  LABELS: a neutral, factual description of the defendant or act said in court (never an insult the court did not use)
  STAKES: the real number or outcome (18 YEARS, $150,000 BOND, 5 YEARS)
  TWIST: what happens next in 2-5 words (and He COLLAPSES, It ENDED BADLY, Full Story)
Rules: 55-90 characters, CAPS on 2-4 power words only, the claim must happen in the first 60 seconds of the video,
no fake quotes, no promise the footage does not keep.
