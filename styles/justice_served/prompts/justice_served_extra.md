  feel = sports scoreboard - every case is a ROUND, the hearing is a matchup (STATE vs the defendant), the closing board is
  the FINAL SCORE. Energetic but factual play-by-play: short sentences, no slang, no emoji, no jokes at a victim's expense.
  Titles follow TITLE RULES (Trial Tales energy).
  - NO CHANNEL INTRO IN THIS STYLE. The video is HOOK -> CASE 1 straight away (the hook ends, the case starts at "calling CR####").
    Treat the intro as 0 seconds everywhere: runtime = hook + cases + recaps/closing. Do not write anything for an intro.
  - WATCH TIME (this channel optimises average view duration): the hook must pose ONE question whose answer comes only at
    the ruling and must never reveal the ruling. Recaps (bridges) end with a one-line tease of the next round
    ("Next round: a mother who lied to the court."). Every case needs at least one coming_up and one watch card.
  - OVERLAYS are shown on screen in this look: fact (KEY STAT; start the text with the number when there is one), so_far
    (PLAY BY PLAY, 2-3 beats separated by " · ", only what already happened), watch (WATCH THIS, <= 6 words, the moment
    happens within 30 s), verdict_q (YOUR CALL, one comment prompt per video), coming_up (COMING UP teaser, ruling stays hidden).
  - video.takes (THE CALL cards, optional, 1 per ~5 min): [{"tc":"H:MM:SS","text":"<=24 words, one plain observation true to the clip","dur":7}]
  - video.exchanges ("ON THE RECORD" cards): the sharpest verbatim Q&A, 1-2 per case of 8+ minutes
    [{"tc":"H:MM:SS","ts":"H:MM:SS","who_q":"PROSECUTOR","q":"<=14 words verbatim","who_a":"WITNESS","a":"<=12 words verbatim","dur":9}]
  - case.proceeding ("JURY TRIAL", "PLEA HEARING", "SENTENCING", "PROBATION HEARING"), case.charge (plain English, <= 6 words),
    case.facing ("2 to 20 years"), case.question (the one question the viewer wants answered, <= 14 words) - all shown on the
    ROUND lower third and THE MATCHUP card.
  - hook.headline: 3-9 words, no period; the most important word may be wrapped in *stars* to get the amber block.
  - hook.quote / hook.quote_by: optional verbatim line (<= 16 words) shown as a quote card on the hook.
  - SUBSCRIBE (mandatory in every video): vo_final MUST include one natural sentence asking the viewer to subscribe
    (e.g. "If you want every round from this courtroom, subscribe to Justice Served and tap the bell.").
