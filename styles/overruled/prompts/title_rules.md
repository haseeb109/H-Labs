 TITLE RULES — OVERRULED  (same five styles, calmer voice for a 40-60 audience)
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Tone: serious courtroom commentary, firm but never cheap. The viewer wants to see
fairness delivered and to understand why. Outrage is allowed; mockery is not.
No slang, no "insane", no "destroys", no "humiliates", no "slaps".

Use the same STRUCTURE and SETUP formulas as Trial Tales (1-10). Payoffs use the
calmer bank:
POWER-VERB BANK
  REJECTS · REFUSES · STOPS · EXPOSES · CALLS OUT · SHUTS DOWN · DENIES · OVERRULES ·
  UNCOVERS · WARNS
EMPHASIS LIST
  EVERYTHING · NEVER · ONE · LAST · AGAIN · SILENT
HOOK WORDS
  lied · probation · times · years · victim · family · plea · deal · record · crash ·
  mother · son · daughter · walk free · second chance

CALIBRATION (rhythm only):
  Broke Probation 13 Times and Asked for One More Chance — {JUDGE_NAME} REFUSES
  "I Didn't Do Anything Wrong" — Then {JUDGE_NAME} Read His Record Out Loud and STOPS Him
  Everyone Expected a Plea Deal — Until {JUDGE_NAME} UNCOVERS What He Told Police
  Mother Pleads With {JUDGE_NAME} for Her Son — The Courtroom Went SILENT
Length, censoring and all TITLES CORE hard rules apply unchanged.
