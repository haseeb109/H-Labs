 v10 TITLES — FIVE TITLES PER VIDEO, FIVE DIFFERENT STYLES
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
Write "titles": exactly five objects {"style","text","ctr_rank"}, one per style,
and mark exactly one {"primary": true, "primary_why": "<one line>"}.
ctr_rank = your CTR prediction order 1-5 (1 = best), all different.

STEP 1 - extract the hook points from THIS case's transcript (do not print them):
  CRIME (specific: victim type, severity, place) · NUMBERS (years, times, $, BAC,
  prior cases) · ATTITUDE (arrogant, lying, begging, smiling, repeat offender,
  outsmart attempt) · JUDGE'S MOMENT (the single most brutal or surprising line) ·
  TWIST (record surfaced, lie exposed, plea rejected, unexpected mercy) ·
  HUMAN MOMENT (family, victim, apology, tears).

STEP 2 - one title per style:
  1 karma   Outrage -> catharsis. [SETUP] — [{JUDGE_NAME} PAYOFF with ONE bank verb].
            Use the SETUP and PAYOFF formulas in TITLE RULES.
  2 quote   Starts with a REAL quote from the transcript in double quotes, at most
            8 words, said by the person you imply. "<quote>" — <{JUDGE_NAME} payoff>.
  3 number  Starts with a number that is in the transcript ("13 Probation
            Violations…", "$40K Stolen…", "11 Years of Crimes…").
  4 twist   Curiosity gap: what everyone expected vs the hidden turn
            ("Everyone Thought She'd Walk Free — Until {JUDGE_NAME} Pulled Her
            2019 Case"). Only if the case has a real turn. If not, write
            style "twist_fallback": a karma title on a DIFFERENT setup+payoff pair.
  5 human   Empathy and drama: a family/victim/defendant emotional beat
            ("Mom Begs {JUDGE_NAME} to Spare Her Son — The Courtroom Went Silent").

HARD RULES (all five):
  · 65-95 characters including spaces (never over 100), about 8-15 words.
  · The hook is inside the first 40 characters (mobile shows only that).
  · {JUDGE_NAME} appears exactly once in every title.
  · Exactly ONE ALL-CAPS emphasis word (acronyms like DWI, BAC, CPS do not count).
    karma uses a verb from the POWER-VERB BANK; other styles use the bank or the
    EMPHASIS LIST in TITLE RULES.
  · Separators: at most one " — " and at most one " | ". No colon. No question
    mark. No emoji. Asterisk only to censor inside a word ("Se* Offender").
  · Pronouns and roles, never the defendant's name.
  · Never reveal the verdict (no sentence length, no "gets probation", no
    "found guilty"). Tease it.
  · Every number and every quote must be in this case's transcript. A script checks
    this and sends the title back if it is not.
  · The five titles must not look alike: different first three words, different
    main words. End on the payoff, never on the setup.
  · Avoid "you won't believe" and other saturated phrases.
